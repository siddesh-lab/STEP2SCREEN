## 🖼️ High-Resolution Prototype Highlights

### Fig 01. Complete Hardware Prototype
![Complete Hardware Prototype](images/hardware_prototype_overview_1793602478676.jpg)
* **File:** `images/hardware_prototype_overview_1793602478676.jpg`
* **Description:** Complete breadboard integration displaying the ESP32-C3, ADXL345 accelerometer, SSD1306 OLED, 5V relay module, and NEO-6M GPS receiver neatly routed with jumper cables.

### Fig 02. Walking & Step Detection Field Test
![Walking & Step Detection Test](images/adxl345_step_detection_walking_1793602474657.jpg)
* **File:** `images/adxl345_step_detection_walking_1793602474657.jpg`
* **Description:** Handheld field test demonstrating footfall impact detection. Dynamic delta filtering ($\Delta > 2.5\,\text{m/s}^2$) and 400ms cadence debouncing distinguish genuine footsteps from casual arm shaking.

### Fig 03. GPS Outdoor Tracking & Velocity
![GPS Outdoor Tracking & Distance](images/neo6m_gps_outdoor_tracking_1793602481978.jpg)
* **File:** `images/neo6m_gps_outdoor_tracking_1793602481978.jpg`
* **Description:** Field evaluation on an outdoor running track under open sky. The NEO-6M module locks satellite constellations to compute real-time walking distance (0.85 km) and velocity (4.2 km/h) over UART1.

### Fig 04. 0.91" OLED Display & 20s Progress Bar
![OLED Display & 20s Progress Bar](images/oled_display_macro_1793602478614.jpg)
* **File:** `images/oled_display_macro_1793602478614.jpg`
* **Description:** Close-up macro of the 128×32 monochrome SSD1306 OLED screen showing speech bubble quotes, a 4-frame stickman animation, step countdown counter (142s), and a bottom-row 20-second accumulator progress bar.

### Fig 05. 5V Relay TV Power Control (Active-LOW)
![Relay TV Power Control](images/relay_tv_power_control_png_1793602484986.jpg)
* **File:** `images/relay_tv_power_control_png_1793602484986.jpg`
* **Description:** 5V relay switching television power. When `stepCount > 0` and the relay sense line (`GPIO 6`) is grounded, the ESP32 drives `GPIO 2` LOW, energizing the relay coil and lighting the green IN1 status LED to power the TV.

### Fig 06. School Science Fair Exhibition Booth
![School Science Fair Exhibition Booth](images/science_fair_pedometer_booth_1793602519436.jpg)
* **File:** `images/science_fair_pedometer_booth_1793602519436.jpg`
* **Description:** Exhibition display booth ready for evaluation. Includes a trifold presentation board displaying the circuit schematic and state machine flowchart, the physical breadboard prototype, and a laptop running the live HTML simulator.
