Everything It Does

Click a card to learn more

👟

Real Walk Detection

ADXL345 uses dynamic peak detection (delta > 2.5 m/s²). Wrist shakes and tilts are rejected. Only genuine footfall impacts are registered.

⏱️

20-Second Speed Limit

Walk 20 continuous seconds to earn 1 TV second. The accumulator pauses when you stop and resumes from where it left off — no progress lost.

📺

Relay TV Control

5V relay physically cuts power to your TV when steps hit zero. Active-LOW on GPIO 2. Relay plug status detected via GPIO 6 sense line.

🛰️

GPS Tracking

NEO-6M GPS tracks real distance (m/km) and speed (km/h). Steps only count when GPS detects ≥ 0.5 km/h movement. Falls back indoors automatically.

💾

Flash Memory

Steps + walking progress saved to ESP32 NVS flash. Survives power cuts. On boot, the device shows "Steps saved: X" and restores your full progress.

🖥️

Animated OLED

Walking stickman with 4 animation frames. Motivational quotes. 20s progress bar. Alternates between step count and GPS distance every 3 seconds.

🔴 Power (Red)

3.3V for ADXL345, OLED, GPS. 5V VBUS for relay coil only.

🔵 I2C Bus (Blue/White)

Shared I2C bus on GPIO 4 (SDA) and GPIO 5 (SCL). ADXL345 at 0x53, OLED at 0x3C.

🟡 Relay (Yellow/Green)

GPIO 2 = Active-LOW control. GPIO 6 = sense line wired to GND on relay board.

🩵 UART GPS (White/Cyan)

GPIO 0 (RX from GPS TX) and GPIO 1 (TX to GPS RX). HardwareSerial(1) at 9600 baud.

System Flow

How It Works — Step by Step

From your footstep to the TV turning on

1

👟 Footfall detected by ADXL345

Reads X/Y/Z acceleration at ~50Hz. A dynamic low-pass filter isolates the spike. When dynamicDelta > 2.5 m/s² and 400ms debounce passes → footfall registered.

2

🛰️ GPS validates movement

If NEO-6M has a satellite fix, checks speed. Under 0.5 km/h → user is stationary → accumulator gated. No fix (indoors) → falls back to ADXL345 only.

3

⏱️ 20-second accumulator fills

walkingAccumulatorMs increments each millisecond of active walking. At 20,000ms → 1 TV step awarded. Remainder carries over. Pauses when you stop.

4

💾 Saved to NVS flash

Both "steps" and "walkAcc" written to flash instantly when a step is earned, and every 5 seconds during walking.

5

🔌 Relay sense pin checks (GPIO 6)

INPUT_PULLUP on GPIO 6. Relay plugged in → reads LOW. Unplugged → reads HIGH. Countdown only runs when relay is physically connected.

6

📺 TV powered via relay (GPIO 2)

When relay connected AND stepCount > 0: GPIO 2 → LOW (Active-LOW) → relay closes → TV on. Every 1 second: stepCount--.

7

⛔ Steps hit zero — TV off

When stepCount == 0: GPIO 2 → HIGH → relay opens → TV cuts off. OLED shows "Walk 2 get steps!" Go walk more!
